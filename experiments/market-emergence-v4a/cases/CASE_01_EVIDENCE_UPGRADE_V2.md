# Case 01 Evidence Upgrade v2

**As-of:** 2026-10-02 completed U.S. session  
**Purpose:** distinguish competing mechanisms with better real evidence.  
**Authority:** research and simulation discrimination only; no causal, trading, sizing, or execution authority.

## What changed

Case 01 v1 established that the semiconductor group had strong common movement and large stock-level dispersion. v2 adds three discriminating evidence layers:

1. event-aligned one-minute price/volume around the 08:30 ET U.S. employment report;
2. a single-name expectation benchmark using Micron guidance versus event-time consensus;
3. ETF activity / AUM proxies that test, but do not identify, a common-flow explanation.

## Timing result

Equal-weight NVDA / AMD / AVGO / MU / AMAT / LRCX:

| Window | Theme | SPY | QQQ |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| 08:29 → 08:35 ET | +0.72% | +0.37% | +0.44% |
| 08:29 → 09:00 ET | +1.04% | +0.42% | +0.57% |
| 08:29 → 09:30 ET | +0.42% | +0.30% | +0.37% |
| 09:30 → 10:00 ET | +0.39% | +0.01% | +0.26% |
| 09:30 → 16:00 ET | -0.19% | -0.07% | -0.06% |

The move started immediately after the macro release and much of the initial gain occurred before the regular-session open. The theme then failed to show persistent positive accumulation from the open to the close.

This supports the **timing relevance** of the macro/rates relief channel, but timing is not causal identification.

## Expectation evidence

Micron's Q1 FY2027 guidance midpoint was:

- EPS: 38.15 versus event-time consensus 36.02, about +5.9%;
- revenue: 61.5B versus event-time consensus 56.77B, about +8.3%.

The public analyst-action sample also contains several post-event target increases.

This is real expectation evidence for **MU**, but not a synchronized estimate-revision panel for the whole semiconductor group.

Therefore the correct status is:

**Theme information repricing: PARTIAL_SINGLE_NAME_SUPPORT**

not “proved for the theme.”

## Common-flow evidence

For SOXQ from Oct. 1 to Oct. 2:

- price: 100.97 → 103.41;
- AUM: 3.235B → 3.310B;
- price-adjusted AUM change: approximately -3.2M, or about -0.10% of prior AUM.

SOXQ Oct. 2 volume was about 0.99× its 30-day average. SMH volume was about 1.16× its 30-day average.

The available proxies therefore do **not** show an obvious large semiconductor-ETF creation wave on Oct. 2.

But this cannot rule out institutional common flow through other ETFs, single names, derivatives, baskets, or internal crosses.

Therefore the correct status is:

**Common theme flow: NO_SUPPORT_FROM_AVAILABLE_ETF_PROXY**

not “flow disproved.”

## Investment-useful conclusion

The 2026-10-02 semiconductor move looks less like a simple day-long common-buying episode than the daily close-to-close return alone suggests.

A stronger description is:

\[
\boxed{
\text{macro-timed premarket repricing}
+
\text{theme strength}
+
\text{large cross-sectional dispersion}
+
\text{no direct evidence yet of persistent common flow}
}
\]

This is a better target for the simulator: reproduce the **observable fingerprint**, not a predetermined story.
